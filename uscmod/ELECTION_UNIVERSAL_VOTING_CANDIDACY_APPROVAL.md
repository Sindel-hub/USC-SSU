# Election Voting and Candidacy Eligibility

- Every active, approved, school-provisioned student account may vote while the official Voting phase is open.
- Voter-roster `eligible` flags no longer exclude an active student from voting.
- Candidate registration is separate and requires `users/{uid}.candidacyApproved == true`.
- The System Administrator controls candidacy approval from User Directory.
- Candidate registration is also limited by the official Candidate Registration schedule.
- Changing a student to an inactive standing removes portal access and therefore voting/candidacy access.
